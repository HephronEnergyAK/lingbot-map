The official KITTI page identifies its datasets and benchmarks as
CC-BY-NC-SA-3.0. Redistribution therefore requires attribution, is limited to
non-commercial use, and requires derivatives to use the same license.

The LingBot Map release corpus uses a derived Continuous Take made from the
first 3,001 left grayscale camera frames of odometry sequence 00. It processes
every selected frame in order without temporal sampling. The derived media is
not checked into this repository, is not included in the Blender Extension or
Worker packages, and does not inherit the repository's GPL or Apache license.
It remains separately governed by `CC-BY-NC-SA-3.0`.
