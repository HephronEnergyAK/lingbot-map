Capture guidance

Record a substantially static scene as one forward-moving, uninterrupted take. Move smoothly, keep useful overlap between adjacent views, avoid featureless or reflective regions where possible, and minimize moving people, vehicles, animals, foliage, and screens.

Version one does not detect or remove Dynamic Content. Moving geometry may produce ghosts, trails, or duplicates. Sky Masking removes only sky point candidates and is not Dynamic Content removal.

Continuous Take only

Hard cuts, transitions, reversed or repeated segments, and speed remapping are unsupported. Edited footage may create camera jumps and incorrect geometry. The Worker does not silently detect, split, or restitch edits. Variable frame rate with strictly increasing presentation timestamps is supported and is not itself an edit.

Lens and projection classes

The source must be an ordinary square-pixel rectilinear pinhole video. Fisheye, 360-degree equirectangular, anamorphic, non-square-pixel, and automatically lens-corrected projections are outside version one. Convert them externally before use; the Extension does not claim reliable projection detection.

Media contract

Choose one local MP4 or MOV containing at least eight progressive, opaque presentation frames. The displayed resolution, orientation transform, and SDR BT.601 or BT.709 color description must remain constant. Interlace, alpha, unsupported HDR such as PQ/HLG/Dolby Vision, corrupt frames, ambiguous video tracks, image folders, multiple videos, and live camera streams are rejected.

Every presentation frame is processed in order. Audio is not decoded or imported. The video is identified by complete SHA-256 but is never copied into the Result or packed into the .blend.
